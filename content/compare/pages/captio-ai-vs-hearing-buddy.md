faqs:
  - q: "Is Hearing Buddy really free?"
    a: "Its live captions are: unlimited, with no minute meter, no account and no card. Share Captions, translation inside shared sessions and saved transcripts are free too. Buddy+ adds summaries, solo translation, Apple Watch name alerts and Boost Audio. Captio AI's free plan gives 100 free credits a day and 3 AI summaries in total."
  - q: "How much does Buddy+ cost in the US?"
    a: "The US App Store listing says US$14.99 a month or US$89.99 a year (October 2026), with 20 hours a month of cloud Enhanced Accuracy. Hearing Buddy's website lists US$9.99 a month, US$59.99 a year or US$7.99 a week. Captio AI Plus costs US$14.99 a month or US$79.99 a year on the US App Store (October 2026), with no free trial."
  - q: "Is there a Hearing Buddy app for Android?"
    a: "No. Hearing Buddy is made for Apple devices only and says there is no Android version or web app. Captio AI is on Google Play as well as the App Store."
  - q: "Does Hearing Buddy work offline on a plane?"
    a: "Yes. Its standard captions run on the phone, so they work with no signal, and translation works once the language is downloaded. Captio AI needs internet for live captions; offline it keeps recording and transcribes when the phone reconnects."
  - q: "Can Hearing Buddy translate Spanish into English?"
    a: "Yes. Spanish is one of its 8 caption languages, and English one of its 10 reading languages, translated on the phone. It is free in a shared session; on your own it needs Buddy+. Captio AI captions and translates 60+ languages live, with a two-way mode."
  - q: "Does Hearing Buddy show who is talking at a family dinner?"
    a: "In a shared session, each person's words appear by name from their own phone. On one phone, Hearing Buddy's own pages describe Speaker Labels in Buddy+ that colour up to four voices and reset after each session. Captio AI labels each speaker from one phone. On iPhone, Recognize Voices shows the names of people whose voices you saved, live and in saved recordings, and on iPhone 16 and later Speaker Direction shows which side of the table a voice comes from."
  - q: "Does Hearing Buddy send my conversations to the cloud?"
    a: "Not by default: speech recognition runs on the phone and audio and transcripts aren't collected. The optional Enhanced Accuracy mode streams audio to a cloud speech-recognition provider while it is on. Captio AI processes speech in the cloud after you agree; its privacy policy says audio is not stored on its servers and your content is never used to train AI models."
  - q: "Can Hearing Buddy caption phone calls or FaceTime?"
    a: "Not on iPhone: Hearing Buddy says iOS doesn't let microphone apps reach call audio, and it points to Apple's Live Captions. Its privacy policy says the Mac version can caption calls playing on a Mac. Captio AI doesn't caption calls either; it is made for speech in the room around you."
