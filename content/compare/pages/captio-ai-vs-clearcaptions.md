Captio AI, for conversations in the room, is free with 100 free credits a day in any country, and Captio AI Plus removes the limit."

    a: "ClearCaptions doesn't describe that. Its pages and terms describe captions for phone calls, and only the words of the person on the call are captioned. For speech in the same room you need a live caption app such as Captio AI, which captions people around you through the phone's microphone."
  - q: "Is there a ClearCaptions app for Android?"
    a: "None was found. ClearCaptions says its mobile app needs an iPhone or iPad, and no ClearCaptions app showed up on Google Play in October 2026, though its terms of use still mention Android. Captio AI is on Google Play, for conversations in the room."
