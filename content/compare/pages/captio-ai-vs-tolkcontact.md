faqs:
  - q: "Is the Tolkcontact app free?"
    a: "For most deaf and hard of hearing people in the Netherlands, yes: every health insurer pays the subscription, with no eigen risico, usually from 35 dB of hearing loss. Calls through KPN Teletolk and to 112 are free, and Tolk op afstand uses your UWV interpreting hours. Captio AI's own free tier is 100 free credits a day of captions; past that, an employer or school can buy licences for its deaf and hard of hearing people, or you take Captio AI Plus (US$79.99 a year on the US App Store, October 2026)."
  - q: "How do I get interpreting hours (tolkuren) from UWV?"
    a: "You apply at UWV, which decides how many hours you get; for private situations it is 30 hours a year (168 if deafblind), requested once and then renewed automatically, and a first request needs an audiogram. UWV then creates your account on mijn.tolkcontact.nl. Captio AI needs no application: you install it and start captioning."
  - q: "What do I need to sign up for the Tolkcontact app?"
    a: "An application at tolkcontact.nl/app with your name, address, date of birth and citizen service number (BSN), plus proof of deafness such as an audiogram or a GP's letter for the insurer, unless you already have UWV interpreting hours. Captio AI asks for none of this: sign in with Apple or Google, or skip."
  - q: "Can I call 112 with the Tolkcontact app?"
    a: "Yes. The 112 button calls the emergency centre straight away; you speak or type, the operator always answers in text, and your live location is shared. For 112 in sign language, call through KPN Teletolk between 7:00 and 22:00. Captio AI can't call 112 or anyone else."
  - q: "Is a schrijftolk or Captio AI better for a job interview?"
    a: "For a planned interview, a booked schrijftolk gives you a trained person who writes everything said, who said it and the sounds in the room, paid from UWV interpreting hours if you have them. Captio AI is automatic: useful when no interpreter is free, and it keeps the recording so you can reread the questions afterwards."
  - q: "Can I use Tolk op afstand in a meeting at work?"
    a: "For short meetings and one-to-one talks of up to 20 minutes, yes. Tolkcontact says remote interpreting suits meetings with many people less well, because the interpreter can't see who is speaking, and suggests booking an interpreter for longer ones. Captio AI captions the whole meeting from one phone and labels each speaker, by name on iPhone for people whose voices you saved."
  - q: "Can I use the Tolkcontact app on holiday abroad?"
    a: "Tolk op afstand works anywhere in Europe, Tolkcontact says, but it isn't sure about outside Europe, and its interpreters are Dutch. Captio AI works wherever you have internet and translates live between 60+ languages, from a market in Spain to a museum in Germany."
