faqs:
  - q: "Is Rogervoice free in France?"
    a: "Yes, if your operator is Orange, SFR, Bouygues Telecom, La Poste Mobile or one of their brands: since 1 October 2026 they pay for 5 hours of calls a month, renewed on the 1st (3 hours before that date, as the French App Store listing still says). The operator Free isn't on the list; without an eligible operator, plans cost €5.99 a month for 1 hour or €29.99 for unlimited calls, and the MDPH and Agefiph can reimburse them. Captio AI's free plan gives 100 free credits a day for conversations in the room, in France and every other country."
  - q: "Is Rogervoice free in the US?"
    a: "Yes, for US citizens or residents with hearing loss who register: the FCC pays for the service, and Rogervoice has been FCC-certified since March 2024. Registration needs a Social Security number and a mobile number with a US area code. The US App Store also lists paid plans at US$5.99 a month for 1 hour of calls or US$29.99 for unlimited calls (October 2026). Captio AI doesn't caption calls; for conversations in the room its free plan gives 100 free credits a day."
  - q: "Can Rogervoice caption a face-to-face conversation?"
    a: "Yes. Live captions, added in version 6.1.0 on 26 October 2026, shows what people around you say in real time; it needs iOS 26 or later on iPhone and works on all Android phones. Rogervoice's pages don't yet say which languages it supports, whether it shows who is talking, or whether it costs anything. Captio AI captions conversations in the room in 60+ languages, labels who is talking, and runs on iOS 15.1 or later and Android. At a family dinner, Captio AI on iPhone can show who is talking by name, and on iPhone 16 and later which side the voice comes from."
  - q: "Can Rogervoice translate a call from Spanish to English?"
    a: "No. Rogervoice's help centre says it doesn't translate conversations from one language to another; you choose the caption language for each call. Captio AI translates conversations in the room live, but it doesn't caption phone calls."
  - q: "Does Rogervoice offer French Sign Language (LSF) interpreters?"
    a: "Yes, for calls in France through the relay offer paid by operators: an LSF interpreter by video, a Cued French (LfPC) coder or a braille-compatible mode. It runs Monday to Friday 8:30 to 21:00 and Saturday 8:30 to 13:00, within 5 hours a month, for calls to France and its overseas departments. Captio AI has no sign language."
  - q: "Can I use Rogervoice in the UK?"
    a: "Yes. The UK is on Rogervoice's lists of countries where you can download it, buy a plan and receive calls. The UK App Store lists £5.49 a month for 1 hour of calls, £27.49 for unlimited calls and 2 hours of extra credit for £9.99 (October 2026). Captio AI is available in the UK too, for conversations in the room; its prices differ by country."
  - q: "How many languages does Rogervoice caption?"
    a: "Rogervoice's international site and US App Store listing say 100+ languages for calls; its French listings say more than 80, and its help-centre list from 2021 covers 64 languages in 119 language and region versions. It doesn't say which languages Live captions supports. Captio AI captions and translates 60+ languages."
  - q: "Can Captio AI caption my phone calls?"
    a: "No. Captio AI captions speech in the room around you, picked up by the phone's microphone, and doesn't caption phone or video calls. For calls you need a call-captioning service such as Rogervoice, so you may want both."
