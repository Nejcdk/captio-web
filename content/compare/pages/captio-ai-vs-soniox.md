    a: "US$19.99 a month or US$119.99 a year on the US App Store (October 2026); Soniox's help centre lists the yearly plan at 50% off. Pro is unlimited and can't be shared with family. Captio AI Plus costs US$14.99 a month or US$79.99 a year on the US App Store (October 2026), with no free trial."
