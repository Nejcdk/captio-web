faqs:
  - q: "Can Google Translate be used as captions for deaf people?"
    a: "Google doesn't describe Google Translate as a captioning app for deaf or hard of hearing people. Its Transcribe and Live translate modes show speech as text while translating it into another language, and Transcribe can show the original words too. Captio AI is made to caption speech in the room for deaf and hard of hearing people."
  - q: "Does Google Translate work offline on iPhone?"
    a: "For text, yes: download a language and it translates without internet. Google's offline live translation is only for Pixel 9, 10 and 11, in 10 language pairs with English. Captio AI needs internet for live captions; without it, it keeps recording and transcribes when you're back online."
  - q: "How many languages does Google Translate's Live translate support?"
    a: "Google's help page lists 73 languages for Live translate. The Transcribe mode supports 8, says the Google Play listing, and typed text translates between up to 249 languages, says the App Store listing. Captio AI captions and translates speech in 60+ languages."
  - q: "Can Google Translate transcribe a lecture in English?"
    a: "Google describes Transcribe as translating a lecture or speech in another language as it happens, and you can save the transcript. It doesn't describe captions in the same language, and Google's 2020 post said Transcribe works best with one person speaking at a time in a quiet room. Captio AI captions a lecture as it is spoken and keeps the recording with its audio."
  - q: "Does Google Translate show who is speaking at a dinner or in a meeting?"
    a: "Google doesn't describe speaker labels in Google Translate; Live translate takes turns between two languages. Captio AI labels each speaker and, on iPhone, can show the names of people whose voices you saved."
  - q: "Can I use Google Translate at a doctor's appointment in Spanish?"
    a: "Live translate can translate a two-language conversation, such as Spanish and English, out loud and on screen. Google doesn't describe adding medical words. Captio AI lets you add medical terms, translates live, and keeps the recording so you can read the appointment again."
  - q: "Does Google Translate's Live translate work in the UK and Europe?"
    a: "Google's Live translate help page lists no country limits. Google's blog says Live translate started in the US, India and Mexico in August 2025, and Live translate with headphones reached the UK, France, Germany, Italy, Spain and more in March 2026. Captio AI is on the App Store and Google Play worldwide."
  - q: "Is Google Translate free?"
    a: "Yes. The App Store lists it as free, and neither store lists in-app purchases (US listings, October 2026). Captio AI is free for 10 minutes of captions a day and 3 AI summaries in total; Captio AI Plus costs US$14.99 a month or US$79.99 a year on the US App Store."
