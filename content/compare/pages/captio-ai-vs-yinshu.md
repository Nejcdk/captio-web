faqs:
  - q: "Is Yinshu free, or do I have to top up?"
    a: "The download is free. The China App Store lists in-app top-ups from CNY 8 to CNY 98 (November 2026), and release notes mention timed translation, a yearly card and extra time for watching videos, but Voibook's pages don't say how much you can use without paying. Captio AI has no top-ups: you get 100 free credits a day at no cost, and Captio AI Plus lifts the limit for US$14.99 per month or US$79.99 per year (US App Store prices, October 2026)."
  - q: "Is Yinshu on Google Play?"
    a: "Voibook's own Android download link goes to Tencent's MyApp store, and the Google Play address for its Android package returns no listing. On iPhone it is on the App Store, also outside mainland China. Captio AI is on the App Store and on Google Play."
  - q: "Does Yinshu caption Cantonese for users in Hong Kong?"
    a: "Its pages don't say which dialects its 2023 dialect service covers. Captio AI captions Cantonese with Traditional Chinese characters, and Mandarin in Simplified or Traditional characters."
  - q: "Do I need a Chinese phone number for Yinshu?"
    a: "You need a mobile phone number to create an account, according to its privacy policy; it doesn't say the number has to be Chinese. WeChat, Weibo, QQ or Apple sign-in can be linked. Captio AI needs no phone number: you can sign in with Apple or Google, or skip signing in and use it on one phone."
  - q: "Where does Yinshu keep my data?"
    a: "Its privacy policy says personal information collected in China is stored in China, with speech handled through iFlytek and AISpeech SDKs, and it lists advertising SDKs. Offline recognition is also available. Captio AI also relies on the cloud, and only after you agree; its privacy policy keeps your audio off Captio AI's servers and rules out using your content to train AI models."
  - q: "Can Yinshu help a deaf child practise Mandarin pronunciation?"
    a: "Yes. That is one of its two main parts: AI scores the child's pronunciation in pinyin, words, sentences and whole texts. Captio AI is a caption app for reading other people's speech, so for pronunciation practice Yinshu is the one built for it."
  - q: "Can Yinshu caption a WeChat video call?"
    a: "Its pages describe captions in Yinshu's own video calls between users who follow each other, and floating captions for livestreams and Douyin, but not WeChat calls. Captio AI doesn't caption phone or video calls at all."
  - q: "Can Yinshu let me know the doorbell rang while I'm reading captions?"
    a: "Voibook doesn't describe sound alerts anywhere. In Captio AI, Recognize Sounds on iPhone and iPad catches a knock, a doorbell or a smoke alarm during a recording, puts it into the transcript in brackets, and vibrates the phone for those alerts."
