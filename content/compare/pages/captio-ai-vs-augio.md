faqs:
  - q: "Who makes Augio, and is it Danish?"
    a: "Yes, it is Danish. Augio ApS in Glamsbjerg makes it for Danish conversations and people with hearing loss, and sells it on the Danish App Store with prices in kroner. Captio AI captions Danish as one of 60+ languages and has its own Danish page at captioai.app/danish."
  - q: "Can Augio replace a skrivetolk (writing interpreter)?"
    a: "No, and Augio says so itself: it is a supplement, not a replacement for a hearing aid, a cochlear implant or a writing interpreter. In Denmark you can apply for writing or sign-language interpretation, for example at work, at the doctor's or for a family celebration (see borger.dk). Captio AI is automatic too and doesn't replace an interpreter; it is one more tool next to them."
  - q: "How much does Augio cost in Denmark?"
    a: "The regular price is DKK 149 a month or DKK 1,500 a year with VAT (Danish App Store, November 2026). Subscribe before 1 January 2027 and DKK 99 or DKK 999 stays your price for as long as the subscription runs; on augio.dk you can pay by card or MobilePay. In the US App Store, Captio AI Plus was US$14.99 monthly and US$79.99 yearly in October 2026, while its free tier of 100 free credits a day costs nothing."
  - q: "Does Augio run on Android, iPhone and iPad?"
    a: "On Android and iPhone (iOS 16.4 or later), plus Windows computers; the App Store lists it for iPhone only, not iPad. Captio AI runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, but has no Windows app."
  - q: "Does Augio understand jysk and other Danish dialects?"
    a: "Augio says it is made for Danish with dialects, numbers and everyday language, without naming the dialects. Captio AI's Danish page lists Rigsdansk, Sjællandsk, Fynsk, Jysk and Bornholmsk, and says very heavy Bornholmsk features may reduce accuracy."
  - q: "Where does Augio keep my conversations?"
    a: "Encrypted on your own device only. The audio sent for speech recognition isn't stored, and the terms say transcripts are kept only if you turn that on. Captio AI saves recordings in a searchable library synced to your account; the audio it processes live isn't kept on its servers, and nothing you record is used to train AI models."
  - q: "Can Augio caption a YouTube video or a DR programme on my PC?"
    a: "On a Windows PC, yes: Augio's App Store listing names online meetings and video as what the computer app is for. On the phone it isn't described. Captio AI doesn't caption sound playing on a device; instead it transcribes audio and video files you upload, with speaker labels."
  - q: "Do I need an account to use Augio?"
    a: "Yes. You sign in with your email and a one-time code, with no password, and the same account works on all your devices. With Captio AI an account is optional: sign in with Apple or Google to sync, or skip it and use the app on one phone."
