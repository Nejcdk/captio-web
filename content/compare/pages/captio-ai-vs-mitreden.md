faqs:
  - q: "Is Mitreden made for grandparents with hearing aids?"
    a: "Yes. Its description names grandparents, people with hearing aids, hard of hearing and deaf people, and everyone who talks with them, and it keeps the screen to a red button and large white text on black. Captio AI is made for deaf and hard of hearing people and lets you adjust text size, background colour and contrast."
  - q: "Does Mitreden work in English?"
    a: "Yes, as long as the iPhone or iPad itself is set to English, since Mitreden listens in the device's language, German or English. Captio AI handles 60+ languages and keeps up when a speaker drops from German into English halfway through a sentence."
  - q: "Can I use Mitreden at the doctor's in Germany?"
    a: "Its description names the doctor's visit as one of the places it is for. It needs internet there, and afterwards keeps only the last 10 texts on the device. Captio AI also needs internet for Live Captions, keeps the appointment as a recording with its audio, and can turn it into a summary with to-dos."
  - q: "Does Mitreden send my conversations anywhere?"
    a: "To Apple, for speech recognition: the recording is sent to Apple and processed there. The developer receives neither the recording nor the text, and there is no account, no ads and no analytics. With Captio AI the speech leaves the phone as well: once you agree, a third-party recognition service handles it in the cloud, and Captio AI's privacy policy promises the audio isn't stored on its servers and your words never train AI models."
  - q: "Is Mitreden available in Austria and Switzerland?"
    a: "Yes. The Austrian and Swiss App Stores list it free as well (November 2026), for iPhone and iPad. Captio AI can be downloaded in all three countries, from the App Store or from Google Play, with prices set per country."
  - q: "Can the person sitting opposite read Mitreden's text?"
    a: "Yes. The button with the turning arrow, bottom right, flips the text 180° so whoever sits across the table reads it the right way up. Captio AI's pages don't describe a flipped view for the person across the table."
  - q: "Can Mitreden translate for relatives from abroad?"
    a: "Not on its pages. For a cousin who speaks French or Arabic, Captio AI's Live Translator shows the translation beside the original words, and in two-way mode each of you reads the other in your own language."
  - q: "Do I need an account for Mitreden?"
    a: "No, Mitreden has no accounts. Captio AI can be used without signing up too, on one phone; signing in with Apple or Google adds syncing."
