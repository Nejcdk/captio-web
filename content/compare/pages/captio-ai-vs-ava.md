faqs:
  - q: "Does Ava have a free plan?"
    a: "Yes. Ava's pricing page and App Store listing describe unlimited basic captions in sessions of up to 40 minutes that you can restart; an older answer on the pricing page still mentions 5 free hours a month. Premium captions, captioning in a language other than your phone's, and translation need a paid plan. Captio AI's free plan gives 100 free credits a day and 3 AI summaries."
  - q: "How much does Ava cost in the US?"
    a: "Ava Community costs US$14.99 a month or US$119.99 a year on the US App Store (October 2026), with 3 hours of Premium captions a month and extra hours at US$4.99. Pro and Enterprise prices are not published. Captio AI Plus costs US$14.99 a month or US$79.99 a year on the US App Store (October 2026)."
  - q: "Does Ava work offline on a plane?"
    a: "On mobile, yes: Ava's offline mode works in solo mode, only in your default language, with somewhat lower accuracy, and not on web or desktop. Captio AI needs internet for live captions; offline it keeps recording and transcribes when the phone reconnects."
  - q: "Can Ava caption Spanish or translate Spanish into English?"
    a: "Ava's help centre lists Spanish among its 23+ caption languages and English with Spanish as a translation pair. Captioning in a language other than your phone's language, and translation, need a paid plan with Premium captions. Captio AI captions and translates 60+ languages live."
  - q: "Does Ava show who is talking at a family dinner?"
    a: "Yes, when each person joins from their own phone by QR code or link: up to 25 people, each in their own colour. On one phone, Ava's SpeakerID (up to 5 speakers) is a Premium-only beta you must be given access to. Captio AI labels each speaker from one phone, live and in saved recordings."
  - q: "Is Ava available outside the US, for example in the UK or Australia?"
    a: "Ava's help centre lists 24 countries, including the UK, Canada, Australia and much of Western Europe and Latin America. Ava Connect for video calls is listed only for Canada, France and the US. Captio AI is on the App Store and Google Play worldwide."
  - q: "Can Ava caption phone calls or Zoom meetings?"
    a: "Not phone calls: Ava's help centre lists phone calls, FaceTime and WhatsApp as things its mobile captions cannot caption. On a computer, Ava Connect captions Zoom, Teams and Meet calls. Captio AI does neither; it is made for speech in the room around you."
  - q: "Does Ava use my conversations to train its AI?"
    a: "Ava's privacy policy (January 2025) says it uses audio segments and their transcribed text to train its speech recognition if you consent; an older help article says it learns how people sound, not what they said. Captio AI's privacy policy says your content is never used to train AI models, and audio is not stored on its servers."
