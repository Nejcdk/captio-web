faqs:
  - q: "Do Captify glasses work with an Android phone?"
    a: "Yes. Captify says its app needs iOS 16 or later or Android 12 or later, and the phone has to stay within Bluetooth range of the glasses. Captio AI also works on iPhone and Android, with no glasses: the captions show on the phone screen."
  - q: "How much do Captify glasses cost in the US?"
    a: "On Captify's US store in October 2026, Captify Pro is US$849 and Captify Light US$499 at regular price (on sale for US$699 and US$399 in late October 2026). The app is free, and a US$15 a month Premium plan is announced as Coming Soon. Captio AI needs no hardware: Captio AI Plus is US$14.99 a month or US$79.99 a year on the US App Store, and the free plan gives 100 free credits a day."
  - q: "Do Captify glasses work offline, on a plane or abroad?"
    a: "Yes for captions: Captify has an offline model, with unlimited free offline captions in 30+ languages. Its help centre says live translation needs internet. Captio AI needs internet for live captions, and transcribes recordings made offline once you reconnect."
  - q: "Can Captify glasses translate Spanish to English?"
    a: "Captify lists live translation into 80+ languages, shown in the glasses, and both Spanish and English are among its languages. A two-way mode is not described. Captio AI translates live in 60+ languages, with a two-way mode where each person reads the other in their own language."
  - q: "Do Captify glasses work at a family dinner or in a meeting?"
    a: "Captify says Captify Pro works best one-on-one, at family gatherings and in small groups, and is not ideal when several people speak at once or in very loud places. Its pages don't describe remembering people's voices. Captio AI captions the whole table from one phone and labels each speaker. On iPhone, Recognize Voices shows the names of people whose voices you saved, and on iPhone 16 and later Speaker Direction shows which side of the table a voice comes from."
  - q: "Can I get Captify glasses with my prescription?"
    a: "Yes. Captify offers single vision, reading and progressive lenses, from -20.00 to +6.00 with astigmatism on Captify Pro, and new prescription lenses start at US$99. Captio AI shows captions on your phone screen, so there are no lenses to choose."
  - q: "Can I use FSA, HSA or insurance to buy Captify in the US?"
    a: "Yes. Captify accepts FSA and HSA cards at checkout, and vision insurance that covers out-of-network glasses may pay you back. Captio AI has no government funding programme; organisations can get licences through captioai.app/organizations."
  - q: "Does Captify caption phone calls?"
    a: "Captify's comparison page lists phone call transcription for Captify Pro, but no Captify page explains how it works. Captio AI is made for speech in the room around you, not for phone or video calls."
