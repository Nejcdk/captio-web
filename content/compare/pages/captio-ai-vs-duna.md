faqs:
  - q: "Can I use duna on a Mac in a classroom in Barcelona?"
    a: "Yes, if the Mac has Apple silicon and macOS 26: duna's Mac app is made as a big, calm screen for a room, a class or a meeting, and works offline once the language is downloaded. Captio AI is made for iPhone, iPad and Android; its App Store listing also lists Apple silicon Macs."
  - q: "Does duna work in Basque or Galician?"
    a: "Yes, both are among its five languages, marked New; duna says they are the most recent and make more mistakes than the other three. Captio AI captions Basque and Galician too, and follows a speaker who switches between either one and Spanish."
  - q: "Does duna send my voice to a server?"
    a: "No. Voice, text and videos stay on the iPhone or Mac, no audio is written to a file, and its App Store label reads \"No se recopilan datos\" (no data collected). Captio AI works the other way: speech goes to a cloud recognition service once you say yes, Captio AI doesn't store that audio on its own servers, and none of your content goes into training AI models."
  - q: "Can duna make Spanish subtitles for my videos?"
    a: "Yes. Pick a video or audio file, choose its language, and duna writes .srt subtitles on the device, then opens an editor and can export the video with the subtitles burnt in. Captio AI can transcribe the same file and mark who speaks when; you get it back as a transcript to read or share."
  - q: "Can I use duna with AirPods or an external microphone?"
    a: "Yes: the iPhone's own microphone, a wired one or Bluetooth headphones, and duna keeps listening when you switch; on the Mac you choose the microphone in its settings. Captio AI keeps captioning on AirPods or an external microphone too; only Speaker Direction, which reads the iPhone's own microphones, then stays hidden."
  - q: "Do duna transcripts sync between my iPhone and Mac?"
    a: "No: a session made on the Mac stays on the Mac, and one made on the iPhone stays there. Captio AI, once you sign in with Apple or Google, keeps recordings and their audio synced in your account."
