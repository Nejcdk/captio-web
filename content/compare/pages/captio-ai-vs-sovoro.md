faqs:
  - q: "How much does Sovoro Plus cost per month in Korea?"
    a: "On the Korean App Store, Basic Monthly is KRW 9,900 (November 2026); the website shows its own prices only after you log in. Bought through KEAD's assistive technology shop, the tablet bundle lists at KRW 1,013,760. With Captio AI you can stay at zero on 100 free credits a day; heavier use means Captio AI Plus, listed in October 2026 on the US App Store for US$14.99 monthly or US$79.99 yearly, and store prices vary from country to country."
  - q: "Can a deaf student in Korea get Sovoro Plus through school?"
    a: "The SK Happiness Foundation says some provincial education offices support Sovoro Plus as an assistive device, and that it was chosen for education office and NIA assistive device programmes; ask your education office or special education support centre. Captio AI isn't part of a Korean public programme, but schools can buy Captio AI Plus licences for students through captioai.app/organizations."
  - q: "Does Sovoro Plus still caption Chinese?"
    a: "No. Chinese captions were removed from the iPhone app in January 2025, and the mobile guide now lists Korean, English and Japanese. Captio AI captions Mandarin and Cantonese among its 60+ languages, and Korean too."
  - q: "Can Sovoro Plus caption a phone call?"
    a: "Not live: its guide says call captions aren't possible. You can upload a recorded call and get captions with speaker separation, within the 10 hours a month for files. Captio AI doesn't caption phone or video calls either; it is made for people talking in the room, and it can transcribe a recording you upload."
  - q: "Can I use Sovoro Plus on a Samsung phone and a PC at the same time?"
    a: "Yes. There is an Android app on Google Play and a web app for PC browsers on the same account, and the mobile app can be signed in on two devices at once. Captio AI runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, with no PC version."
  - q: "Where does Sovoro Plus send my audio?"
    a: "To the cloud. Its privacy policy lists Amazon Web Services and Google LLC in the US as processors, with the audio and video files users enter among the data sent to Google. Captio AI works in the cloud too, once you've agreed to it; under its privacy policy the audio isn't kept on Captio AI's servers, and what you record is never used to train AI models."
  - q: "Which app suits a grandparent in Korea with age-related hearing loss?"
    a: "Sovoro is made with older Koreans with hearing loss in mind: a Korean-only app, caption colours, sizes and fonts to choose, and Korean phone support. Captio AI captions Korean too, with large adjustable text, a free plan of 100 free credits a day, and, on iPhone, Recognize Voices so the family's names appear in the captions."
