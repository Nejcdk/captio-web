answer: "It depends on the job. To read what people in the room are saying, use a live caption app: Google Live Transcribe on Android, Apple's Live Captions built into iPhone, iPad and Mac, or an app such as Ava or Captio AI. For videos and calls playing on the phone, the phone's built-in captions do that. For phone calls in the US, call-caption services such as InnoCaption and Rylo are free for eligible users through an FCC programme. Each comparison below shows what an app does, from its own pages."

faqs:
  - q: "What is the difference between Live Transcribe and Live Caption on Android?"
    a: "Live Transcribe captions people talking around you, through the phone's microphone. Live Caption captions audio playing on the phone, such as videos, podcasts and calls, and doesn't caption people in the room. Both are made by Google. Captio AI does the job of Live Transcribe, on Android and iPhone."
  - q: "Which caption apps work on iPhone?"
    a: "Apple's Live Captions is built into iPhone, iPad and Mac, and Captio AI, Ava, Otter.ai, Soniox, Hearing Buddy and Live Transcribe by Mighty Fine Apps have iPhone apps. Google Live Transcribe and Android's Live Caption are Android only; Hearing Buddy and Live Transcribe by Mighty Fine Apps are Apple only."
  - q: "Which caption apps show who is talking by name?"
    a: "Captio AI's Recognize Voices, on iPhone, remembers the people you talk to often: save a voice once and their name shows on every line they say, live and in saved recordings, instead of \"Speaker 2\". On iPhone 16 and later, Captio AI's Speaker Direction also shows which side of the table a voice comes from. Otter.ai learns voices you tag and names them after the recording stops. Ava's SpeakerID (a Premium beta) and Hearing Buddy's Speaker Labels tell voices apart on one phone; Hearing Buddy's labels reset after each session."
  - q: "Which caption apps work without internet?"
    a: "Google Live Transcribe captions offline in downloaded languages on Android 12 or later, and Android's Live Caption and Apple's Live Captions process audio on the device. Ava has an offline mode on mobile in solo mode. On iPhone, Hearing Buddy and Relay caption on the phone without internet, and Live Transcribe by Mighty Fine Apps has an Offline mode for close-up talk. Captio AI, Soniox and Otter.ai record offline and transcribe once you are back online."
  - q: "Are there free caption apps for deaf people?"
    a: "Yes. Google Live Transcribe, Android's Live Caption and Apple's Live Captions are free, and on iPhone Hearing Buddy and Relay caption for free without a limit in their on-device modes. Ava, Otter.ai, Soniox and Captio AI have free plans with limits, shown on each comparison page, and paid plans for more."
  - q: "Which apps caption phone calls for deaf people in the US?"
    a: "Call-caption services such as InnoCaption, CaptionCall, ClearCaptions, CaptionMate and Rylo (formerly Nagish) caption phone calls, and are free for eligible US users through the FCC's IP CTS programme. Live caption apps such as Captio AI are made for people in the room, not for calls."
